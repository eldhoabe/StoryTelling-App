# Product Design Document: BioGraph Stories

## 1. Vision Statement
A narrative non-fiction web portal for children aged 3–7 that delivers strictly fact-checked, realistic scientific stories. The platform replaces fantasy with the wonders of the natural world, using a "Knowledge Graph" to connect biological and geological concepts.

---

## 2. Core Principles
* **Absolute Realism:** No talking animals, no magic, and no science fiction. Every story is rooted in peer-reviewed scientific fact.
* **Visual Storytelling:** Information is delivered via "Card Stacks" (5–10 cards per story) with realistic illustrations and clear annotations.
* **The Connection Web:** Instead of linear lists, stories are nodes in a graph. Completing one story unlocks related factual paths (e.g., *Dinosaurs* → *Asteroids* or *Birds*).
* **Parent-Child Interaction:** Designed for parents to read aloud, fostering discussion and shared learning.

---

## 3. User Experience (UX) & Design
### 3.1 The Card System
Each story consists of 5–10 digital cards.
* **Front Side:** High-quality realistic graphic + 1–2 simple sentences.
* **Annotations:** Interactive hotspots on the image that reveal deep-dive facts (e.g., "This is a fossilized tooth").
* **Navigation:** Swipe left/right to progress.

### 3.2 The Knowledge Graph (Navigation)
* Visual map of interconnected nodes.
* Completed stories are highlighted.
* New stories "branch out" from the completed ones based on shared facts.

---

## 4. Content Prototype: "The Day the Giants Left"
**Focus:** The K-Pg Extinction Event (How Dinosaurs became extinct).

| Card | Visual Description | Key Scientific Fact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | A lush Cretaceous forest with a Triceratops. | 66 million years ago, Earth was home to giant reptiles. |
| **2** | A large asteroid glowing in the atmosphere. | A 10km wide asteroid (rock from space) entered the atmosphere. |
| **3** | A massive impact in water (Chicxulub crater). | The asteroid hit the ocean, causing massive dust clouds. |
| **4** | A dark, cold landscape with withered plants. | Dust blocked the sun for years. Without sun, plants could not grow. |
| **5** | Small feathered dinosaurs and mammals in a burrow. | Small animals survived because they needed less food. |
| **6** | A modern bird (Sparrow) next to a fossil. | Birds are the only dinosaurs that survived to the present day. |

---

## 5. Knowledge Graph Connections (Phase 1: Animals)
* **Story: Extinction**
    * *Link (Space):* **Asteroids & Meteorites** (How rocks move in space).
    * *Link (Biology):* **The First Birds** (How feathers helped survival).
    * *Link (Botany):* **Sunlight & Food** (How plants eat).

---

## 6. Content Integrity Protocol
1.  **Fact-Check Loop:** Every story must be cross-referenced with educational scientific databases (Smithsonian, NatGeo, etc.).
2.  **No Anthropomorphism:** Animals must be depicted in their natural behaviors; no human clothing or speech.
3.  **Terminology:** Use correct terms (e.g., *Theropod*, *Atmosphere*) but explain them through context and parent guides.

---

## 7. Roadmap
* **V1 (MVP):** 10 Animal/Nature stories, static Knowledge Graph, Parent-read mode.
* **V2:** Audio narration, interactive annotations, "Collection Room" for discovered facts.
* **V3:** Expansion into Geography and Physics (strictly non-fiction).
